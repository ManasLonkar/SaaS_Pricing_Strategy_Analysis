**SaaS Pricing Strategy Analysis**
An interactive Power BI project analysing SaaS subscriptions,
customer behaviour, revenue, discounts, and pricing-plan performance.

**Project Overview**
This project uses a synthetic SaaS dataset to explore business
performance and support data-informed decisions about subscriptions and
pricing.

**Dashboards**
1. Executive Overview --- Revenue, active subscriptions, customers,
   and monthly trends.
2. Customer Behaviour --- Subscription activity, upgrades,
   downgrades, and plan changes.
3. Revenue & Discount Analysis --- Revenue by plan, discount
   patterns, and monthly trends.
4. Pricing Recommendations --- Pricing-plan comparisons, revenue
   contribution, and subscription performance.
   
**Tools & Skills**
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modelling
- Data Analysis & Visualisation

**Business Use**
The dashboards help explore revenue trends, compare pricing plans,
understand customer plan changes, and review discount patterns. These
insights can support decisions by management, finance, marketing, and
customer-success teams.

Note: This is a portfolio project using synthetic data. Findings
are illustrative and do not represent an actual company's performance.
